Slopoverse is a channel of original comedy series: cutout-animation satire, live-action movie parodies, fake prestige TV and sketches. Every video is written by a person and produced with AI tools; the faces, voices, music and motion are generated, and every upload is labeled as AI-generated.

Public figures appear only as exaggerated comedic impressions of their public personas. All dialogue is invented, and nothing in our videos is real footage or real audio.

Slopoverse Publisher

Slopoverse Publisher is our in-house tool. It uploads short clips of our own episodes to our own social media accounts on a schedule. It is not offered to the public, and it doesn't sign in, read or post for anyone else.
